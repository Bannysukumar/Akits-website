OBC Cell

Policy Implementation:

The OBC Cell works within the framework of the OBC reservation policy, which provides for reservations in educational institutions and government jobs for OBC categories. The cell plays a crucial role in ensuring that these policies are implemented effectively and that OBC students and staff benefit from the provisions.

Overall, the OBC Cell serves as a vital resource for promoting social justice and equity within educational institutions and government bodies by ensuring that OBC communities have equal opportunities to access education and employment.
