HOD PROFILE

Sri G.KRISHNA MOHAN,B.Tech, M.Tech,(Ph.D) is the department’s head. He has been teaching in Electronic and communication for 14 years and has produced several research publications in both national and international journals. The department has highly trained and committed instructors that train students for overall growth. The department offers educational tools for students to create digital and analogue communication, analogue integrated circuits and computing systems. We are the top Electronics and Communication institutions in Kothagudem thanks to the two UG programmes offered by the ECE Department, which was founded in 2009 and has a yearly intake of 60 students for B.TECH (ECE).
