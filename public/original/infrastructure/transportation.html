Transportation

The Abdul Kalam College in Kothagudem is of the opinion that more productive transportation methods increase output.

Private transportation to the college is offered by AKITS through buses from all parts of the district. The fleet transports students to the college and throughout Bhadradri-Kothagudem district from a variety of routes. The people have access to transportation from every part of the city. Buses operate from all locations, catering to the students’ desire for hassle-free transportation while being driven by a team of qualified drivers.

As a result, numerous buses routinely travel to this area. In addition, the college has enough public transport and private autos  on the road to get students to class room on time.The special thing about it is that senior faculty members also take care of the students in the buses, so their needs are met even during the long bus rides.

The only college of its kind where transportation is offered to students on a no-profit, no-loss basis is likely AKITS. For us, transportation is not a business; rather, the students’ comfort comes first. Bus drivers are chosen from experienced candidates whose qualifications have been thoroughly examined. This allows the parents at home to sleep soundly.
