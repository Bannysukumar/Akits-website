anti ragging cell 1

The Discipline and Anti-Ragging Cell, established in accordance with UGC regulations, serves as the moral compass of our institution. Its primary responsibility is to ensure that students uphold discipline within classrooms and across the campus. Members of the Cell conduct regular rounds to monitor behavior and ensure the campus remains free from ragging and eve-teasing. The Cell takes stringent action against students involved in such activities.​

Students can approach any member of the Discipline & Anti-Ragging Cell or any of their teachers to report incidents of ragging or other disciplinary issues. Additionally, grievances can be submitted online by emailing akitsarc@gmail.com.​

It is important to note that filing a complaint is a serious matter; therefore, students are encouraged to exercise this right responsibly. The institution assures that all complaints will be handled with the utmost sensitivity and confidentiality.
