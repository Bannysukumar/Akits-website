STUDENT GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL COMMITTEE(SGRC)

The Grievance Redressal Committee is established to address and resolve complaints and concerns raised by students, faculty, and staff in a fair, transparent, and timely manner.
This committee is mandatory under guidelines from bodies like UGC, AICTE, and NAAC, and must exist in all higher educational institutions.
Objectives of the Grievance Redressal Committee:
1. To provide a platform for stakeholders (students, faculty, staff) to raise grievances related to academic, administrative, or personal matters.
2. To investigate complaints impartially and confidentially.
3. To resolve grievances in a time-bound manner.
4. To promote a positive and supportive institutional climate.
Common Grievances Handled:
• Harassment (including bullying or ragging)
• Unfair academic evaluation or discrimination
• Delay in scholarship disbursement
• Issues related to examination or attendance
• Infrastructure problems
• Administrative inefficiencies
