Cafeteria

Best engineering colleges in Kothagudem offer a canteen which is attractively constructed and offers a large selection of delectable and healthful food options that are cooked in hygienic settings.

The setting is appropriate and delicious. The environment is ideal for students to relax in, and many find the music to be calming.

The campus canteen offers a large selection of sanitary breakfast, lunch, and meals with snacks. The student’s health has been taken into consideration as adequate measures were taken to provide them with safe drinking water. 

South Indian, North Indian, Chinese, vegetarian, and non-vegetarian cuisines are all offered at affordable pricing by the college canteen.
