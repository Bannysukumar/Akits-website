ADMISSION PROCESS AND GUIDE LINES

The primary entrance exam for engineering admissions in Telangana is the Telangana Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy (Veterinary etc.,) Common Entrance Test (TG EAPCET), also known as EAMCET. Admissions are primarily based on merit in this exam, with a portion of seats reserved for management/NRI candidates and other categories like EWS and diploma holders.
Detailed Process:

1. Eligibility:
Candidates must have passed their 10+2 (Intermediate) examination with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as optional subjects.
Specific eligibility criteria may vary for management/NRI quotas, and other categories like EWS and diploma holders.
2. Entrance Exam:
The main entrance exam is the TG-EAPCET.
Candidates need to apply online through the official website during the application window.
The process includes registration, filling details, uploading documents, and paying the application fee.
3. Counseling and Seat Allotment:
After the TG-EAPCET results are released, candidates are called for counseling conducted by the convener.
Seat allotment is based on merit rank in the EAMCET, subject to any reservations.
Counseling is typically held in multiple rounds.

4. Documents Required:
• TS EAPCET Rank Card.
• TS EAPCET Admit Card.
• Intermediate Mark Sheet &Transfer Certificate.
• 4th to 12th Study Certificates.
• Class 10th Certificate.
• Domicile Certificate (if required).
• Caste Certificate (if required).
• Counseling Fee Payment Receipt.
• Income Certificate (if required).
• Aadhar Card

Additional Information:
Lateral Entry:
Diploma holders can be admitted to the second year of B.Tech through a lateral entry scheme based on their merit in the Engineering Common Entrance Test (ECET),.
Reservations:
The government of Telangana provides various reservations for different categories like SC, ST, EWS, etc.,.
Management Quota:
A certain percentage of seats is reserved for management/NRI candidates.
