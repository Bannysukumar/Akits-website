About Our Department

In the year 2009, the MBA Department was established. It is a two-year curriculum that is affiliated with the All India Council for Technical Education and Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Hyderabad (JNTUH) (AICTE). 

It has dedicated faculty members who are highly qualified, experienced, and dedicated. Along with giving their all in the classroom, the department’s students are very eager and actively engage in a variety of co-curricular and extracurricular activities.

Strength of the Department

Lectures, case studies, fieldwork, and group projects are expertly blended to create an integrated teaching-learning phenomenon. We are regarded as one of the top MBA colleges in Kothagudem. The department often hosts seminars, workshops, and conferences on the most recent advancements as well as organises expert lectures, industrial visits, and seminars for the benefit of the students.
